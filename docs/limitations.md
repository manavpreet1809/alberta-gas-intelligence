# Current limitations

- The scope is provisional: facilities in the Kakwa area of Alberta (Twp 60–65,
  Rge 3–7 W6M), January 2024–December 2025, with December 2023 context. The fixed
  551-member candidate cohort is reproducible, but human approval remains pending.
- Facility location is not linked-well origin and does not establish Montney,
  Duvernay or any other formation membership. The box is analyst-selected.
- Public extracts can omit confidential or suppressed records. The metric is
  selected public GAS/PROD contribution, not all physical output in a region.
- No qualifying row is distinct from reported zero. It does not establish shutdown,
  nonexistence or failure to report. Invalid qualifying values fail publication.
- Monthly reporting BA IDs are not ultimate corporate groups or proof of ownership.
  Display-name equality is insufficient to combine IDs. Known reassignments and
  UNKNOWN attribution changes do not establish acquisitions or asset transfers.
- First observed means first in available history. Returning positive does not
  establish a restart. History begins in the selected snapshot and is left bounded.
- The selected snapshots were retrieved on different dates and may reflect provider
  revisions. They are not an as-first-reported series; no revision horizon is assumed.
- Concentration applies to reported BA-ID contributions in this fixed cohort, not a
  legal/economic market. Shares and HHI cannot be summed across months.
- Exact volume reconciliation validates arithmetic and traceability. It does not
  prove the source measures every physical event or that classifications are causal.
- The .pbix, executed DAX checks, screenshots and manual dashboard reconciliation
  are pending. Written findings are provisional analyses of accepted exports.
- Historical reference evidence remains in Git, but ignored reference raw snapshots
  have not been restored locally. Monthly source operators are the primary assignment.
- Source use requires Crown acknowledgement and respects the documented distinction
  between non-commercial and commercial use. No unrestricted redistribution or
  commercial permission is claimed. Intended portfolio publication requires review.
